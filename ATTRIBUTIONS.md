## Data & cartography

**Administrative boundaries (India states & union territories)**
- Source: Natural Earth 10m Admin 1 – States, Provinces.
- Licence: public domain.
- Usage: the choropleth map of datasets-by-state. Simplified and name-normalised
  by `tools/prepare_boundaries.py`.

**Basemap tiles**
- Provider: CARTO (Positron, light) via OpenStreetMap data.
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO.
- Usage: background context for the interactive map.

**Catalogue records**
- The dataset index (`assets/data/datasets.json`) is an **independent curation**
  of metadata that references public data portals and providers (ISRO/Bhuvan,
  Survey of India, Central Water Commission, India-WRIS, IMD, FSI, GSI, Census
  of India, ICAR, NATMO, INCOIS, data.gov.in, OpenStreetMap, DataMeet, Microsoft,
  Google Research, Natural Earth, NASA/CIESIN, USGS, ESA/Copernicus,
  OpenTopography, ICIMOD, KSRSAC, and others). Each record links to its source;
  the source's own terms govern the underlying data.
- Catalogue data licence: **CC-BY 4.0**.

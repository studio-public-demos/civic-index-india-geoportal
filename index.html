How scores are computed

Each portal is scored 0–100 from its catalogued records: open & free access (20), download capability (25), GIS-ready formats (15), API & OGC services (15), data recency (15) and link reliability (10). See methodology.

How it works

An index maintained by Studio, not by hand

The catalogue is assembled from primary sources — government portals, open-data platforms, community data and international repositories — by a pipeline of Studio agents, with human review for uncertain records.

01This is a discovery layer, not a data host. Nothing is re-served; every record links to the source, and the source's terms govern.
02Licence and access are classified from source evidence and can mislead — always confirm at the source before use.
03Provenance first. Every assertion carries its source, evidence and verification timestamp.
04The catalogue is open data — downloadable as JSON/CSV under an open licence.

How scores are computed

Each portal is scored 0–100 from its catalogued records: open & free access (20), download capability (25), GIS-ready formats (15), API & OGC services (15), data recency (15) and link reliability (10). See methodology.

Nebula Civic Index

Civic Index across jurisdictions

Nebula Civic Index is an open public-data intelligence initiative by Nebula Cloud Public Good Labs. It helps make fragmented public datasets easier to discover, verify, compare and act on across jurisdictions. GeoIndia is the first live regional edition.

Public data is abundant but fragmented across agencies, portals and jurisdictions. Civic Index is designed to provide a reusable intelligence layer for discovering what exists, understanding whether it is usable, comparing sources and identifying what is missing. Build locally. Standardize globally.

How it works

Assembled with Studio, reviewed by people

The catalogue is assembled from primary sources — government portals, open-data platforms, community data and international repositories — using a Studio-assisted discovery, classification and verification workflow, with human review for uncertain records. Autonomous scheduled maintenance is part of the platform roadmap.

01This is a discovery layer, not a data host. Underlying datasets are not re-hosted; every record links to the source, and the source's terms govern.
02Licence and access classifications are informational. Confirm source terms before production or commercial use.
03Provenance first. Every assertion carries its source, evidence and verification timestamp.
04The catalogue is open data — downloadable as JSON/CSV under an open licence.
